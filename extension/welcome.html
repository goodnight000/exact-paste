          When it isn't sure
          
It asks instead of guessing: pick a value or paste the original. After every smart paste, Undo and Paste original sit next to the field.

        

        

          Where it works
          
Form fields on HTTPS websites. Chrome's own pages, the Web Store, Google Docs and editors that draw their own text are not supported; use normal paste there.

        

        

          Saved details and AI
          
Both optional. Matching runs on this device with no key or account. In Settings you can save details to fill fields your clipboard can't, and add an AI fallback that sends anything only when you choose it.
