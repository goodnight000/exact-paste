## About your company
Tallyhand turns the receipts a field crew already photographs into job costs a contractor can bill against the same day.

## What are you building?
A phone app for crews and a web ledger for the office. A crew lead snaps a receipt or a supplier invoice. We read the line items, match each one to the open job and cost code, and flag anything that does not fit the estimate before the office closes the week.

Today the office manager does this on Fridays from a shoebox of paper. On our pilot jobs she now spends about forty minutes a week on it instead of six hours, and two of our three pilot contractors caught overbilled lumber orders in the first month.

Next we are adding change orders, so a crew lead can price extra work on site and the client signs it on the same screen.

## Why did you pick this idea?
I ran estimating for my family's remodeling company for six years. We lost money on jobs we thought were profitable because costs showed up three weeks late. Priya built the receipt pipeline for expense reports at Ramp and saw the same lag from the other side. We have been building this together nights and weekends since March.

## How far along are you?
Three paying contractors, 41 active jobs, $2,100 MRR.
